Not every presentation needs slides, but when they earn their place,
 they work as a visual channel that complements the speaker rather than
 replacing them. Sumeet Gayathri Moghe sets out the
 principles that follow from that idea — control over the rate of knowledge
 exposition, tight coupling between slides and speaker, minimalism by
 default, visuals that say more than words can, and never sending your
 slides out in advance.
