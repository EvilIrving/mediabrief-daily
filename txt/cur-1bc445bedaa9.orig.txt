If anyone represents a link between the history of semiconductors and its future, it's Pat Gelsinger. Architect of the landmark Intel 80486 CPU — launched in 1989(!) — he became CTO of Intel and then returned as Intel CEO in 2021 only to leave the firm again at the end of 2024. He didn't disappear from the tech scene, though, and he's now a General Partner at the Venture Capital firm Playground Global.

With decades of experience working in senior positions at the leading edge of chip design he has a unique perspective on both the history, present and future of semiconductors.

Which is why I wanted to share his recent appearance on the a16z podcast. I found it absolutely riveting. After a warm-up covering his early days at Intel he shares his views on a wide range of current 'hot topics'.

It's absolutely worth watching (or listening to — Spotify etc links below) — but if you'd like to dip into individual sections then there are links to each section after the video.
